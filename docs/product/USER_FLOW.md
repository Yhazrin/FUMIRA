# User Flow

1. Launch on the Future Camera poster-style connection screen.
2. Connect the mock rotary controller, or choose phone-only mode.
3. On iPhone, approve the real system camera permission; Simulator explains its fallback.
4. Enter the immersive full-screen live viewfinder and capture a JPEG.
5. Watch AI scan the captured photo for subjects, composition and change clues.
6. Watch the story model connect seven anchors from -100 to +100 years.
7. Review the generated title, logline and current time beat.
8. Scrub freely across ±100 years, then approve or rewrite the story.
9. Generate the selected time-world from the original photo plus the approved story.
10. Browse the story and scene together, save the poster, return or retake.

The model-background button shows each pipeline role and whether a route is ready.
Failure and disconnection states preserve completed work and retry from the failed
stage rather than restarting the full pipeline.
